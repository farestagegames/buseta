## Full description (2056/4000)

```text
Start your shift in Puerto Marisol, a made-up city on Colombia's Caribbean coast, with a seafront, palm trees, a market, roundabouts and brightly painted barrios. Pick up whoever flags you down, collect fares, stop when someone asks to get off and keep to time without knocking anything over.

YOUR BUS
• Start in the Caimán B8 buseta: small, quick and always in a hurry.
• Save up for the longer Caimán B10, the 12 metre busetón, La Chiva with its painted wood, and an 18 metre articulated bus with a real working joint.
• 11 paint jobs inspired by the coast and 5 horns to choose from in the Garage.

THE STREET
• Passengers hail you from any corner, street vendors hop on to sell, and riders ask for their stop.
• Cars, taxis and motorbikes brake, honk, give way at junctions and overtake when the road is clear.
• Traffic lights, speed bumps, potholes and kerbs. Transit police hand out fines, and on Normal and Hard the odd fare dodger sneaks on.

ROUTES AND MODES
• Four city routes, from the Malecón Loop to the Grand Tour.
• La Ola Line: fixed stations on a dedicated busway in the articulated bus.
• Ruta Libre: no end and no depot, for as long as your passengers stay happy.
• Carrera Express: race the clock through checkpoint arches and boost pads.

DAWN TO NIGHT
• Five times of day: dawn, morning, midday, sunset and night.
• Tropical downpours with wet roads, less grip and working wipers. Brake early.

IN THE CAB
• A cockpit view with mirror and hands on the wheel, or outside cameras.
• A radio with four made-up stations playing original cumbia, vallenato, champeta and salsa.
• Steer with an on-screen wheel, buttons or by tilting your phone. Hold-to-brake, D and R gears, indicators and doors.

YOUR CAREER
• 10 drivers to choose from, each with their own story.
• A daily challenge with a streak, 18 achievements, levels and best scores for every route and bus.
• Three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Hard.

Plays offline. No ads and no in-app purchases.
The game is in Colombian Spanish, and in English on phones set to English.
```
