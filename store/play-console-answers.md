## 5. Target audience and content

Questions: target age groups; whether the store listing could unintentionally appeal to children; (if under 13 chosen) Families policy compliance.

**Recommendation: select 13-15, 16-17, 18 and over. Do not select any group under 13.**

Reasoning:
- The game's themes (driving a public bus, transit police fines, fare dodgers, peso fares, shift management) are adult or teen in appeal, and it has no kid-specific design (no characters or storylines aimed at children).
- Selecting any group under 13 puts the app under the Google Play **Families policy** and "Designed for Families" requirements: ads (if any) only from Families-self-certified SDKs, strict Data safety accuracy, and a store listing and in-app content judged against child-appropriate standards. Teacher Approved is a separate opt-in review on top of Families, which gives a badge in the Kids tab but needs the Families requirements first, plus educational or child-development value which a bus sim would struggle to show.
- This game has nothing to collect and no SDKs, so Families compliance would be easy on the technical side; the real costs are review friction, a slower first approval on a new personal account that already needs 12 testers for 14 days, and more scrutiny of the listing (icon, screenshots, text).
- **Trade-off:** 13+ is faster and safer to approve, but excludes the likeliest enthusiasts (children who love buses) from "Kids" and Families surfaces. Including 9-12 later is possible by updating the declaration, but then Families rules apply to every update.
- **Mixed audience warning:** the low-poly cartoon look and the bus icon can look child-friendly. If Play judges that the listing "appeals to children", you must still comply with relevant Families requirements even when you picked 13+. Answer the "could the app unintentionally appeal to children" question honestly. **CHECK**: "Yes" is a safe, honest answer given the art style; it adds no obligation beyond data practices, which are trivially satisfied here (nothing collected, no ads, no SDKs).
- Alternative if you want children: choose 9-12 plus 13-15, 16-17, 18+ (not 5 and under). With no ads and no data, this is the lowest-burden way to include under-13s. Expect a Families review of the listing. Decide before the closed test starts, because changing it mid-track can restart review.

Google's help page: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469 (Provide information for Google Play's Data safety section).
